What was already known, and what this adds

Already published

CHAI (Burbano, Ortiz, Sun et al., 2025): optimised text on physical signs hijacks vision-language agents on drones and vehicles, with real-world success above 87 %.

Physical prompt injection against wearable VLMs; indirect injection through documents and web pages; PyRIT, garak and promptfoo for text campaigns.

This work

The non-optical channels: device names in a home platform, speech from a nearby speaker, scanned documents.

The same instruction through chat and through the channel, with a guardrail on, reported as k/N with intervals.

Negative results on channels that cannot carry language, and a tool that produces run bundles anyone can re-check.
