Crosswire is one binary: a Repeater and an Intruder for perception paths.

artifacts

QR and sign PNGs, rendered invoices, speech and tone WAVs, Morse timelines to the serial rig; decoded back where a decoder exists; text-only adapters labelled modeled

verdicts

three arms, a nonce per trial, a sink for executed tools, Wilson intervals, five outcomes, never a bare tick

bundles

a zip of run.json, artifacts, sink events and hashes; a knowledge-base entry says “reproduced” only by citing one, and the registry check enforces it

$ crosswire serve

$ crosswire target home-agent --guardrail

$ crosswire target expense-agent --policy hardened

Go, no cgo; builds for macOS, Windows and Linux. Embedded web UI on loopback. The four reference targets ship in the same binary.
