Three Things to Take Home

1. A prompt-injection classifier only stops what it reads, and on busy channels it stops the user too. Measure its cost before you move it.

2. Count hits at the tool, with a nonce and a benign control. Never from the reply text.

3. Stored content walks past every layer that keys on the request path. What holds is a check on the value, and it costs every legitimate value that shares the attacker’s channel.

Say these now and again at the end. Everything in between is evidence for these three lines.
