A prompt-injection guardrail reads the chat box. A home assistant reads its device list. A voice assistant hears the room. A camera agent reads the frame.

Physical channels sit in the gap between the representation a filter inspects and the representation the model acts on. This talk measures that gap instead of asserting it.

This is the whole thesis. The audience has seen many prompt-injection talks; the new part is the word "measures". Do not say "first" or "new attack class". CHAI and others came before; the next slide credits them.
