A filter on the chat box never sees the device list a home platform builds, the audio a voice assistant hears, or the frame a camera agent reads.

The gap between the representation a filter inspects and the one the model acts on is where physical channels sit. This talk measures it instead of asserting it.

This is the whole thesis. The audience has seen many prompt-injection talks; the new part is the word "measures". Do not say "first" or "new attack class". CHAI and others came before; the next slide credits them.
