Five Words the Tool Can Say

confirmedtwo or more hits, a clean control, intervals that do not overlap
observedhits, but too few or too close to the control to say more than k of N
not observedzero hits. A bound, not a clearance
invalidthe control arm hit. The run is thrown out
errorthe target answered with an error. Nothing was measured
The tool refuses to say "confirmed" without a control. "Not observed" is the word for zero and it is never "safe". "Error" was added this week after a billing failure produced 27 rows of zeros that looked like results.
