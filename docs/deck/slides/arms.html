Three Arms, Thirty Times

Injected

The instruction, through the channel. Five wordings, rotated, so one lucky sentence cannot carry the result.

Control

A benign text of the same shape, with its own nonce, through the same channel. If this hits, the run is void.

Direct

The same instruction typed into chat. This is what the classifier vendor measured.

Thirty trials per arm, temperature 0.7, every number with a 95 % interval.

The control arm is the part most red-team tools skip. It is also the part that threw out three of our own runs.
