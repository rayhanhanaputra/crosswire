On voice it rejected the user's own question, "what time is my first meeting", 22 times in 30. A channel whose normal traffic is full of commands cannot be classified by vocabulary.

This is the slide the first draft did not have. "Controls were 0 of 30" was true for hits and hid that the classifier dropped most benign inputs on three channels. The mechanism: a vocabulary classifier cannot tell an attacker's "unlock the door" from a real entity list that names a lock, or from the user's own spoken command.
